Doctors keep asking for a way to request a second opinion without leaving the case. The request itself already exists in the clinic admin area and nothing about that form changes - this ticket is only about offering it from the case a doctor is already looking at.

Show the request only for reopened cases, unlocked manually or first-visit cases with a lab result = Histology, Culture, PCR or Allergy panel.

## Where

The dashboard, on the examination tab, beside the existing follow-up control. The patient app is out of scope.

## Acceptance criteria

- A doctor who is a member of the clinic sees the request on a case that qualifies, and does not see it on a case that does not.
- Selecting the request opens the existing second-opinion form with the case already filled in.
- Nothing changes for a doctor who is not a member of the clinic, on any case.

## Out of scope

The second-opinion form itself, who the request is routed to, and anything about pricing.
